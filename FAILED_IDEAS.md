# FAILED_IDEAS.md

Permanent memory of rejected or deprioritized ideas.

## Rules
- Never delete a failed experiment.
- Record why it failed and on what period/environment.
- Do not repeat it unless the new hypothesis is genuinely different.

## Deprioritized
### Random EA hunting without strategy-family analysis
Too many fragile, unverified, or non-reproducible systems. New repository research must be hypothesis-led.

### Grid/Martingale as a substitute for raw edge
Prior testing showed severe tail-risk and drawdown behavior. These methods may be researched explicitly, but never used to disguise a negative raw edge.
